# Changelog

All notable changes to The AI-Ready Brand Standard are recorded here.

The version stamp in `STANDARD.md` and on the web version always match. Revisions land in this repository first.

## v1.0, September 2026

First public release.

- Five Brand Readiness Levels: 0 Absent, 1 Stated, 2 Specified, 3 Governed, 4 Practiced.
- Two tests applied to every rule: the guessing test and the violation test.
- Twelve brand dimensions, each with a Present bar and a Followable bar.
- Level 3 set as the AI-ready floor; Level 4 (Practiced) applied selectively, to the four dimensions where practice matters.
- Weighted scoring by drift risk divided by machine enforceability, in three tiers summing to 100%.
- Two headline numbers: the overall score (weighted mean) and the floor.
- Four tiers: AI-ready, Governed, Specified, Stated.
- Explicit scope limit: the standard measures reproducibility, not brand quality.
