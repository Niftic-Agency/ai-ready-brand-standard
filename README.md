# The AI-Ready Brand Standard

A definition of what it means for a brand to be ready for AI, and a method for measuring it.

Published by [Niftic](https://www.niftic.com). Version 1.1, October 2026. Licensed CC BY 4.0.

**[Read the standard](STANDARD.md)** · [Web version](https://www.niftic.com/ai-ready-brand-standard) · [Why we wrote it](https://www.niftic.com/insights/the-ai-ready-brand-standard)

## What this is

Brands are now reproduced at volume by people using AI, most of whom were never briefed. Whether that output is on-brand depends on whether the brand's rules can be followed by someone with no context.

The standard measures exactly that. It grades twelve dimensions of a brand system on a five-level scale, weights them by drift risk, and reports two numbers: an overall score and a floor.

It does not measure whether the brand is any good. That limit is deliberate and explained in section 1.

## The short version

Every rule in a brand system is graded against two tests:

- **The guessing test.** Could AI follow this rule without guessing?
- **The violation test.** Could you point at a finished piece of work and prove it broke the rule?

And every dimension sits at one of five **Brand Readiness Levels**:

| Level | Name | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Absent | Nothing there |
| 1 | Stated | A description a human interprets |
| 2 | Specified | Exact values, no rules for use |
| 3 | Governed | Behavior written down |
| 4 | Practiced | Governed, plus annotated real work |

Level 3 is the floor for AI-ready. Level 4 applies selectively.

## How to use it

1. Score each of the twelve dimensions from 0 to 4 against the Followable column in section 5.
2. Report the overall score and the floor. Act on the floor first.
3. Score the material a tool actually receives, not the material a person can find.

You do not need any particular software to apply it, and the standard deliberately does not require one. It also does not require a specific document format: it judges the enforceability of whatever shape your brand system already has.
